# Security

 * `lightning-transaction-sync`'s Electrum and Esplora clients no longer
   discard transactions whose witness-stripped serialization is exactly 64
   bytes when syncing confirmed transactions. Genuine mined HTLC claims can
   have exactly this size, and dropping them prevented us from learning the
   preimage needed to settle a forwarded HTLC upstream. Their inclusion is
   verified like that of any other transaction, against the Merkle root
   committed to by the block header.
