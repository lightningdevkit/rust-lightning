# Backwards Compatibility (0.3)
 * Spontaneous payments (keysends) that include a `payment_metadata` TLV in the
   onion are now failed back with `incorrect_or_unknown_payment_details` rather
   than accepted. The metadata of an invoice payment is authenticated against
   the payment secret we generated for it, which spontaneous payments do not
   have, so LDK can no longer surface sender-chosen metadata on them (#5615).
